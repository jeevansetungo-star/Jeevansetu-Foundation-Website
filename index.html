Who we are

Care that sees the person, not just the problem.

JeevanSetu Foundation is a community-focused initiative based around Ramnagar and Varanasi. Our purpose is to help people and families find reliable guidance, understand recovery options and take informed next steps with dignity.

  • ✓Respect first
    Every person deserves privacy, kindness and to be treated without stigma.
  • ✓Family-inclusive support
    We encourage constructive conversations and practical guidance for families.
  • ✓Responsible referrals
    Health-related care should be planned with qualified professionals and appropriate services.
